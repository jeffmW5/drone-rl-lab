Unmodified STL geometry, in meters. Rendered with a -16 mm Z assembly offset.
These are official low-polygon simulation meshes, not manufacturing CAD.
AI/Flow deck shapes added in visuals.py are illustrative, not vendor CAD.
Visual meshes do not set collision geometry, mass, or inertia.
