## Reward16 findings

The native production helper and actual Puffer incoming hard clip were independently read. Reward16 reduces with height error, speed and horizontal displacement in valid near-upright states. Its metre-valued soft absolute has a1cm transition; speed remains squared in m/s. The additional1-minus-square-extent factor deliberately seeks the takeoff center and pays0 at±1m, although the unchanged prototype hover criterion accepts that edge. This is a bundled graded reward change, not merely removal of one clip operation. Compare behavioral outcomes against frozen15 at identical physics5/reset/observations/learner settings; do not compare return magnitudes or claim which component caused any gain.

The square boundaries are continuous in value when ground_cost=0, not differentiably smooth: measured1m slopes are approximately-0.528 on the left and-1 on the right. Abs/max cusps remain at the origin and diagonals. Ground reward retains the15cm gate discontinuity. Immediately below2m on the ground, raw reward is about-1.8 then becomes-1 at failure; actual clipping makes both-1, hiding that raw jump. Thus this does not remove every clipping effect.

A normalized finite quaternion is a precondition. Physics5 normalizes each substep; the reward helper does not validate it. An unflagged NaN quaternion yields NaN reward; a non-unitqx5 can produce out-of-range rewards. These are synthetic invalid inputs, not observed production failures. Failure flags override to-1. No guard or frozen-source edit was made.

Even a valid flipped quaternion has a counterexample: at height0.8m and speed3.5m/s, core is negative. Rewards atX0/0.5/0.9/1m are-0.012745/-0.006724/-0.001400/0, so outward motion inside the zone becomes less negative. Do not claim global inward monotonicity over all attitudes. Whether such states are reached or curtailed by prototype failure is untested.

This is a counterfactual reward profile using measured fields, not another flight. It shows the flipped/high-speed counterexample does not reverse shaping in these known failure states.
